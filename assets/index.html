01 What this is

When decisions feel heavy, clarity often gets distorted by emotion, pressure, and partial information.

This is a structured decision review using the Personal Decision Topology framework. We examine the forces, relationships, pressures, underlying structure, and probable consequence paths shaping your situation.

The goal is simple — and worth stating plainly: understand the full situation before you act.

02 Who this is for

This may be appropriate if you are

  • Facing a consequential decision.
  • Emotionally entangled in a complex situation.
  • Concerned about unintended outcomes.
  • Trying to think clearly under pressure.
  • Wanting structural clarity before committing.

No prescriptions. No pressure.
Just structured clarity.

Understand the full situation before you act.
03 What you leave with

After the session, you will have

  • A mapped understanding of your situation.
  • Identified blind spots.
  • Clarified leverage points and constraints.
  • Modeled consequence paths.
  • A grounded basis for decision-making.
04 The framework

Personal Decision Topology

Grounded in a defined framework, the review examines five structural dimensions of your situation. Select a node to read each in detail.

Dimension 01

Forces at Play

This dimension identifies motivations, incentives, fears, and constraints shaping the behavior of the people involved. It clarifies what is driving movement in the situation, whether stated openly or operating in the background.
