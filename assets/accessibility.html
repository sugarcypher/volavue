Accessibility

Accessibility statement.

Last updated 2026-05-24

Our commitment

We want VolaVue to be usable by everyone, including people with disabilities. We aim to align with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

What we do

  • Semantic HTML and clear heading structure on every page.
  • Visible keyboard focus, and tab navigation through all interactive elements.
  • Sufficient colour contrast for body text and important interface elements (WCAG AA).
  • Respect for the prefers-reduced-motion setting: when enabled, non-essential animation is disabled including the gold sheen on display type and on-scroll reveals.
  • Respect for the prefers-color-scheme setting, with a manual light/dark toggle.
  • Form fields have associated labels; status messages are announced via aria-live.
  • The interactive Personal Decision Topology diagram is keyboard-navigable: tab between nodes, focus previews a dimension, Enter or Space selects.

Known limitations

  • The scheduling flow is provided by Cal.com and the payment flow by Stripe. Their accessibility is outside our direct control; we test them where possible.
  • Some decorative elements (the grain overlay, the metallic-gold sheen on numbers) are designed to be invisible to assistive technology, but they may add small amounts of visual noise.

Reporting a barrier

If you find an accessibility barrier on the site, please tell us at volavue@volavue.fyi. Include the page URL and a short description of the problem. We will respond as soon as we can and aim to resolve issues promptly.
