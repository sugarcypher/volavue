The session

This session applies the Personal Decision Topology framework to your current situation.

Over a single, focused conversation, we will:

  • Map the forces and incentives in play.
  • Identify structural blind spots.
  • Clarify the relational dynamics.
  • Model probable consequence paths.
  • Identify irreversible thresholds.

You will leave with a grounded, structured understanding of your decision environment.
