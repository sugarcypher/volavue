What this Service is, and is not.

Last updated 2026-05-24

What this Service is

Volavue is a structured decision review service that applies the Personal Decision Topology framework to a personal or professional decision you bring to us. A session is a single, contained analysis of approximately 60–90 minutes.

What this Service is not

  • Not therapy or psychological treatment. The Service is not a substitute for licensed mental-health care.
  • Not ongoing coaching. A session is a single contained engagement, not a continuing relationship.
  • Not crisis intervention. If you are in crisis, please contact local emergency services or a qualified professional.
  • Not legal, financial, tax, medical, or other regulated advice. Nothing in a Volavue session constitutes professional advice in any regulated domain. For such advice, consult a qualified, licensed professional.

No outcome guarantees

We do not promise particular outcomes from a session. Decisions, and their consequences, remain yours. The Service is intended to help you see the structure of your situation more clearly so you can make a decision with that clarity in hand.

Your decisions are yours

You retain full responsibility for any decisions you make and any actions you take, before, during or after a session. We accept no liability for outcomes that follow from your decisions.
